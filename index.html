YT Report

A private tool that sends a daily YouTube Analytics summary for the JinpanShorts channel to its owner’s Discord server.

What it does

Once a day, YT Report reads read-only statistics for the channel owner’s own YouTube channel, such as views, watch time, subscribers and traffic sources, and posts a short report with growth suggestions to a private Discord channel.

Who it’s for

This app is used only by the owner of the channel. It is not offered to the public.
